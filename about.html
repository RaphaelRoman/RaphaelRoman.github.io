About

I tell stories through visually pleasing and aesthetic art — and I'd love to help tell yours.

I'm Raphael — a 3D Generalist emphasizing VFX, currently pursuing a BFA in Effects Animation. My work spans cinematic simulations in Houdini, real-time effects in Unreal Engine 5, and product animation for brands and campaigns.


Whether it's a complex fluid simulation, a stylized game ability effect, or a 5-second product loop — I approach every project with the same intent: make something that stops you in your tracks.
